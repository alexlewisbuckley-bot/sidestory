The story
Hotel Lobby began as Story I, commissioned from a novelist and printed on cotton paper before the first accord was weighed. Nine pages arrive with the bottle; the digital edition arrives with your confirmation.
Notes
Top — bergamot, pink pepper. Heart — woods, spice, green. Base — vetiver, cedar, a little smoke. Composed in Grasse by Jacques Chabert.
The stone
Nero Marquina, hand-cut in Liguria. Veining is decided by the block, so no two lids repeat. The lid lifts free of the glass and keeps its weight in the hand.
Delivery & returns
Complimentary UK delivery over £100, otherwise £5. Two to four working days, signed for. Unopened bottles may be returned within 30 days; samples are non-returnable but always credited.

The story behind it

Nine pages, before a single note.

“It was ten minutes before eight when she arrived, and the bar had just begun to forgive the afternoon.”

Every fragrance in the house is written first. A novelist is commissioned, paid, and left alone; only when the pages are finished does the brief go to Grasse. The perfumer works to the writing, not to a mood board.
