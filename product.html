I

The story · Chapter I of IX

The lobby at ten to eight.

written by Morgan Childs — nine pages, printed and boxed with this bottle

It was ten minutes before eight when she arrived, and the lobby had already decided the evening for her. The revolving door gave its slow, museum turn; the marble took her heels and made them sound deliberate. At the bar, the wood had been polished so long it had opinions.

She asked for nothing yet. The barman, who understood waiting the way sommeliers understand rain, set down a glass of ice and let it speak.
