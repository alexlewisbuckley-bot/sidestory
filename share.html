Why we are asking

We have never made a fragrance from a marketing brief. We are not about to start.

Every bottle we make was composed against nine pages of writing. Those pages have always come from our own writers — but the best moments we’ve heard this year came from the people wearing them: a bakery at 5am, a hospital corridor at Christmas, a borrowed jumper that still smelled of someone. One of those deserves a bottle.

How it works

Four steps, one bottle.

I

Write it plainly

No more than 500 words. A moment, not a memoir — where you were, who was there, what the air was doing.

II

We read everything

Every submission is read by the house. You will hear from us either way, within a month.

III

A shortlist goes to Grasse

Four stories a year are sent to Jacques Chabert and the Argeville noses to be read aloud, unattributed.

IV

One becomes a fragrance

Published under your name in the printed edition, with the first bottle of the run sent to you.

What we are looking for

Air, weather, rooms, hands, hours. The specific over the sweeping — one Tuesday beats a whole decade.

And what we are not

Product reviews, note lists, or anything you would call content. Write it as though no one is buying.
