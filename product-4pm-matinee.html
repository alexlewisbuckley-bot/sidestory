The story · Chapter I of IX

The four o’clock showing.

written by Morgan Childs — nine pages, printed and boxed with this bottle

Nobody goes to the cinema at four in the afternoon unless they are hiding from something. The foyer knows it and is kind about it: the carpet takes your footsteps, the girl on the desk does not ask, and the room beyond is already dark.

There were nine of us. We sat with an empty seat between each, like a code, and for two hours the afternoon was somebody else’s problem.

citrus in a dark room — velvet, dust, and an afternoon spent elsewhere
