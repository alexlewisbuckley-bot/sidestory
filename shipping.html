Returns
Unopened bottles may be returned within thirty days of delivery for a full refund. Email contact@sidestoryparfums.com and we send a prepaid label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of arrival.
Samples
Samples and The First Lines are not returnable, for reasons we hope are obvious. The cost is always credited against your first full bottle instead.
Damaged in transit
Stone travels well but not perfectly. Photograph the parcel as it arrived and write to us the same week; we replace without argument.
Gifts
Gifts bought in November and December may be exchanged until 31 January. The invoice goes to the buyer by email — nothing in the parcel mentions price.
