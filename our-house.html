The house

We write the story first.

Nine pages of fiction, commissioned and paid for before a single note is weighed — then a perfume composed to keep it.

Home / Our House

Side Story began with an argument about briefs. Every fragrance house we had worked with started from a mood board — a page of adjectives, a photograph of a beach, a competitor to beat. We wanted to start from something a person had actually written, and had been paid properly to write.

So we commission novelists. They are given a feeling, a length, and no notes at all on scent. When the nine pages come back we send them to Grasse, and the perfumer works to the writing — to the hour of day in it, the room, the weather, the thing left unsaid.

The result goes into a bottle with the story printed alongside it, on cotton paper, in an edition that matches the run. The lid is cut from a single block of stone, so the veining on yours has never existed before and will not again.

The stones

No two lids repeat.

Each lid is cut from a block chosen for its seam, not its evenness. We do not select for consistency and we do not correct the veining, which means the lid on your bottle is the only one of its kind. It lifts free of the glass and is heavy on purpose.
